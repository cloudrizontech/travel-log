# Agra 

**July 1–3, 2025**

The Taj Mahal was every bit as spectacular as we had envisioned. 

The main reason we came to Agra was, of course, the Taj Mahal.

And it was worth it.

Our stay at The Oberoi Amarvilas elevated our trip into an unforgettable experience. 

Something truly extraordinary.

---

## Logistics & Arrival

* **First Night Arrival:** We arrived in Agra late in the evening around 7:00 PM and spent our first night at **Taj Hotel Agra**. Its convenient location made logistics effortless, allowing us to enjoy a lovely property and dinner before officially starting our sightseeing.

* **Transit Strategy:** We flew Air India from **Mumbai to New Delhi** and took a private car to Agra. For future trips, we recommend pre-booking all ground transit in advance through trusted hotels or established, reliable providers.

---

## The Taj Mahal

Standing in front of the Taj Mahal is a transcendent experience. Photos simply cannot capture its scale, flawless symmetry, white marble brilliance, and intricate inlay work.

Leaving betwee **5:00 - 6:00 AM** was essential for beating the July heat, allowing us to experience the monument in a calm, cool, and comfortable atmosphere.

---

## Agra Fort

Agra Fort really brings history to life. You walk past these enormous red sandstone walls into sweeping courtyards filled with detailed white marble design. 

The absolute highlight is the view: you turn a corner, and there’s the Taj Mahal sitting beautifully right on the horizon.

---

## Accommodation: The Oberoi Amarvilas

The Oberoi Amarvilas formed the emotional heart of our trip. 

Beyond the frame-worthy Taj Mahal view from every room, the serene gardens, live traditional music, and folk dancers created an enchanting atmosphere.

* **Morning Elegance & Departure:** 

Before our early morning visit to the Taj Mahal, we were treated to a morning spread in an extraordinarily elegant, grand room with a direct view of the Taj. 

The quiet sophistication of the space and the team's meticulous attention to detail made starting the day feel truly special.


* **Seamless Access:** 

The hotel's private golf buggies whisked us directly from the lobby to the monument gates, allowing us to glide past the busy approach streets and enjoy a peaceful, uninterrupted walk up to the Taj.

* **World-Class Service:** 

The hospitality was quiet, intuitive, and deeply sincere. 

Our fondest memory remains our departure: the staff gathered outside to wave us off, keeping their eyes on our car until it turned the corner. That genuine warmth stayed with us long after Agra was behind us.


* **In-House Dining:** 

We dined at the hotel restaurants each evening.

 Exceptional cuisine combined with live performances and distant views of the Taj Mahal turned every meal into a highlight.

### Would We Stay There Again?
* **Verdict:** Absolutely, in a heart beat.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
* **Taj Mahal & Sites:** Visit early morning (especially in summer) to beat the heat and crowd, explore Agra Fort, Mehtab Bagh and Tomb of Itmad-ud-Daulah (The Baby Taj).

* **Stay & Dining:** Consider staying at Amarvilas to fully enjoy the property, or Taj Hotel Agra for a convenient, cost-effective alternative.

* **Logistics:** Pre-book all ground transportation in advance through trusted hotels or reliable providers.

### Don't
* **Shopping:** Skip driver-led store stops and unsolicited shop recommendations unless you specifically intended to shop—never feel obligated to buy.

* **Unsolicited Offers:** Avoid engaging with unsolicited guides, street vendors, or photographers

* **Transport Scams:** Never rely on hailing unvetted private cars on arrival; keep transport arranged through verified channels.
